Common questions

FAQ.

The questions clients usually ask before we start — answered upfront.

How fast can you start, and how do you reply?

I can usually start within 3 days of a signed scope. WhatsApp / email replies typically within ~2 hours during Cairo working hours (GMT+2 / GMT+3 DST), and same-day otherwise.

How long does a typical project take?

Shopify stores: 2–4 weeks from kickoff to launch. Theme customization: 1–2 weeks. Laravel apps: 4–8 weeks for a focused MVP, longer for multi-tenant SaaS. API integrations and automations: 3 days to a week.

Are you available full-time, or only freelance?

Both. I'm currently open to full-time roles (remote or Cairo) and to freelance / contract work. If you're hiring, send the JD via email and I'll reply with my notice period and availability.

How do payments work?

For freelance: a 50% deposit on signing, 50% on delivery. Larger projects can be split across milestones. I send a simple written agreement covering scope, timeline, and payment terms — bank transfer or InstaPay.

What's included after launch?

Every project includes a 2-week post-launch window for bug fixes and small tweaks at no extra cost. After that, I offer monthly retainers or pay-per-task — whichever fits how often you need changes.

Do you work alone or with a team?

Most projects I deliver end-to-end on my own — that's why I can move fast. For larger builds I bring in trusted designers / QA / DevOps as needed, but you always have one point of contact: me.

What if my project isn't Shopify or Laravel?

I also work with WordPress, Magento, PrestaShop, and Python (Telegram bots, automations). If a tool isn't a fit for what you need, I'll tell you upfront — even if it means recommending someone else.

Have a project in mind?

Tell me about it — Shopify storefront, Laravel platform, API integration, or full-time role. Replies within ~2 hours, and I can usually start within 3 days.
