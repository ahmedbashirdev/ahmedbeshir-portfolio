Talk to the hotel, not the front desk.

A guest with a question shouldn't have to walk down to a counter. Chat puts the whole hotel one tap away — and routes each conversation to the people who can actually answer it.

  • Three channels, one inbox

    The guest can message hotel staff, message hotel management, and — behind the scenes — management talks to staff. Three threads, each going to the right people.

  • Chatting straight with staff is faster

    A quick word with the front desk gets a request moving sooner than a phone call or a trip to the lobby — the team replies in minutes.

  • A complaint that reaches a manager

    If something goes wrong, the guest takes it straight to management, who can fix it fast — heading off a bad public review and lifting how the stay is remembered.

  • Management sees how staff perform

    The management↔staff thread speeds coordination and gives managers real oversight — who is responding, how quickly, and where help is needed.

  • Tied to service requests

    A message can create or track a request, and the live card sits right in the thread — guest, staff and management all see the same status.

A note for the hotel

Every message is stored per hotel and shown to each person in their own language. The chat connects guests, staff and management in one place — and links straight to the service requests they are talking about.
