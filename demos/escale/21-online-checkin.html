Checked in before you arrive.

The slow part of any stay is the first ten minutes — the queue, the form, the card. Online check-in moves all of it to the days before arrival. The guest steps out of the car and walks straight to the room.

  • Skip the front desk entirely

    A real competitive advantage. The guest finishes check-in on the phone before arriving and goes straight to the room — no queue, no waiting.

  • Everything settled before arrival

    Identity is verified, guest details are confirmed, and payment is taken — all done quietly before the trip, so nothing is left to sort out at the door.

  • Room and key issued early

    The room is assigned in advance and the QR digital key is on the phone, ready. The guest knows exactly where they are going.

  • The first door-open tells the hotel

    When the key opens the door for the first time, management sees it in real time — the guest has arrived and the room now shows as occupied.

  • Works for any booking channel

    Not only Escalé bookings. A guest who booked through an OTA or the hotel directly can still check in online, as long as the hotel links the reservation.

A note on the lock

The digital key is a smart-lock vendor integration. Escalé issues the pass — the QR code shown here — but the actual unlock depends on the hotel's lock hardware. The check-in flow stays the same; only the lock connector changes per property.
