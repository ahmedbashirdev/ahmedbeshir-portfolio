Why this screen matters.

Screen 14 is the hotel's side. This screen is the travel agency's side. The agency asks for rooms one time, hotels send their prices, and the agency picks the best offer. No other app in Saudi Arabia connects the two sides.

01

One request, all hotels

The agency fills a short form one time. It reaches every hotel in the city together — no phone calls and no emails.

02

Hotels send clear prices

Each hotel replies with a real price and what is included. The agency sees all the offers next to each other.

03

The cheapest offer is on top

The lowest price gets a pink "Best price" label. The agency picks fast — one look, one click.

04

You can see who to trust

Each hotel shows its stars, its guest rating, and how many groups it has hosted before.

05

The full price is clear

Every offer shows the total price, not only the price for one night. No surprises later.

06

One pink button

"Accept offer" is the only pink button on the screen. Pink always means the final decision.
