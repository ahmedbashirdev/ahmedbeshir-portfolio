A room in one glance.

Housekeeping moves fast — the cart is rolling, the radio is on. The screen has to keep up. It is made to be glanced at, not read.

"The colour of the band tells me everything. Then I read the number."
  • A colour strip on the edge

    Every room card has a coloured strip on the side. You see the status before you even read the number. Yellow means act, green means move on.

  • One button per card

    Each room has just one button — Start, Done, Inspect, Later, or View. The button colour matches the next step.

  • Tap to see more

    A tap opens three quick actions: mark clean, report an issue, add a note. Most of the time, one tap is enough.

  • Guest details when they matter

    "Next arrival 16:30" tells the cleaner how much time they have. A guest name makes the room a person, not a number.

  • Scan a QR to jump

    Scan the door tag and the screen opens that room's card. The fastest way when you are already at the door.

  • Progress shown at the top

    "8 of 12 cleaned" with a bar. Staff can feel how their morning is going.

How a room changes status

A room moves through fixed steps: dirty, in progress, clean, inspected, ready. Each step does something automatically — for example, marking a room clean tells the supervisor to inspect it. The screen only ever offers the next correct step, so no one can pick a wrong status.
