Made for the people doing the work.

Staff apps are often hard to use. This one is fast and clear, and it respects the worker's time.

01

Big room numbers

The room number is large, so staff can read it in one second.

02

Your shift at the top

Staff see how much time is left in their shift and whether they are on track.

03

Calm priority marks

A thin red line marks an urgent task. No flashing and no loud banners.

04

Buttons by colour

Each action has its own colour, so staff learn the colour, not the word.

05

The guest is a person

Each task shows the guest's name and details, not just a room number.

06

A clear record

A timeline shows everyone who worked on the task. No arguments later about who did what.
