The aesthetic

Editorial hospitality with a North African soul. Warm earth tones ground sophistication. The brand reads as a calm, considered space — not luxury intimidation, not budget compromise.

  • Editorial typography · Fraunces variable serif paired with Geist sans gives every screen magazine gravity.
  • Warm earth palette · Aubergine and terracotta evoke Mediterranean hospitality; magenta sharpens what would otherwise be quiet.
  • Geometric motif · The three-triangle mark from the logo recurs as decorative cue across photography overlays and dividers.
  • Generous space · Layouts breathe. Asymmetry over symmetry. White space treated as a primary element.
