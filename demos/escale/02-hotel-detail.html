Why this works

This is the page where a visitor decides to book. Everything on it is here to help the guest tap Reserve and feel sure about it.

  • Photo first, words second The big photo fills most of the first screen. A guest trusts a real picture before any text.
  • One room suggested Two room choices, with one clearly marked as the best fit. Too many choices slow people down.
  • Reserve button always visible The Reserve bar stays on screen while scrolling, and shows the price, so the guest is never lost.
  • Reviews look like quotes A guest review is shown like a nice quote, not an angry rating. It sets a warm tone.
  • Amenities as icons Four small icons instead of a long list. The eye reads them instantly.

Parts we reused

  • Big photo card Used on any screen that leads with a photo.
  • Rating pill The same small rating tag used across the app.
  • Room card A normal one and a "recommended" one. Easy to add more later.
  • Sticky action bar The bottom bar used on the booking, payment, and service screens.
A note about photos

The coloured placeholder shows the photo style we want — warm, golden light, with deep colours in the shadows. Real photos should match this warm look. Avoid cold, bluish stock photos.
