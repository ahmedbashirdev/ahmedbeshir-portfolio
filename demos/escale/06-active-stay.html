The key is the product.

Most hotel apps hide the QR key deep inside. We made it the main thing on the screen. That one choice is what makes Escalé feel different straight away.

"I tap, and the door opens. I tap, and breakfast appears. The hotel reorganised itself around my pocket."
  • A "wow" in 2 seconds

    When the screen opens, the colourful key card is the first thing you see. It also looks great in a screenshot.

  • The screen is about your stay

    Your room number, your breakfast time, the weather, the nights left — all about this stay, not general features.

  • The concierge is always there

    Mariam's banner reminds the guest a real person is ready to help. It calms first-time visitors.

  • Six services, easy to reach

    Spa, restaurant, room service — all one tap away. Easy access means guests use them more.

  • Activity feels like care

    "Towels refreshed 1h ago" shows the guest the hotel is looking after them, even when they don't see staff.

How the digital key stays safe

The QR key is a secure code that only this guest's phone can show. The door reader checks it without needing internet. The code changes every 30 seconds, so a screenshot of it cannot be reused.
