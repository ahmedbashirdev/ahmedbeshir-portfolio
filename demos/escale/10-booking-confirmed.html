A calm, clear confirmation.

Most apps celebrate a booking with confetti and big "SUCCESS!" banners. We kept this calm, like a quiet handshake. The guest just paid real money — the screen should feel trustworthy, not loud.

  • Green means paid

    The check mark uses our success green. It is a trust signal, not a party.

  • It names the destination

    The title says "You're going to El Gouna", not a booking code. The code stays quietly below.

  • The booking card is a keepsake

    A nice photo with clean details. The guest will screenshot this and share it.

  • Four useful actions

    Add to calendar, book a transfer, message the hotel, share the trip — exactly what the guest needs next.

  • A clear timeline of what's next

    After booking, people wonder "what happens now?". We answer with 4 steps, each with a time.

What happens after you book

After payment, the system sends four things automatically: the confirmation email right away, a hello from the concierge within a day, a pre-arrival pack five days before, and the QR key on arrival day.
