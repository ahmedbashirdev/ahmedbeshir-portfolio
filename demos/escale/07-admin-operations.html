Why this screen is different.

Most hotel dashboards look the same and feel cold. This one is clear and warm, and easy to read at a glance.

01

Big, clear numbers

The important numbers are large and easy to read, even from across the room.

02

One number stands out

Only the occupancy number gets the coloured card. It is the number that matters most today.

03

All rooms in one look

Every room shows on one grid, with a colour for its state. The manager sees everything fast.

04

Calm, not noisy

Requests use a thin colour line instead of loud red labels. The screen stays calm.

05

See who checked in by phone

A pink label shows which arriving guests already checked in from their phone.

06

Guest reviews shown clearly

The latest guest review appears the same way the guest wrote it, easy to read.

07

The room tells the desk it is in use

When a guest opens the door with the QR key, the dashboard knows at once that the room is occupied.
