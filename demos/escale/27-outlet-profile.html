A shop inside the hotel.

Every service in the app lives somewhere — a restaurant, a spa, a salon, a maintenance desk. That place is what this page is about, and the services hang off it. Some places belong to the hotel. Some are separate businesses renting space inside it. The guest shouldn't have to work out which.

  • The place first, then its services

    A haircut, a massage, a table — each one belongs to a place inside the hotel. That place has a name, a page, and a price list. This screen is that page.

  • The hotel's own, or someone else's

    Some places are run by the hotel itself. Others are known names renting space inside it, like this studio. We say so plainly at the top, and everything else stays the same — same app, same booking, same total.

  • Some things need a time, some don't

    A towel goes up whenever the guest asks. A haircut takes a chair and a person for an hour, so it needs a date and a time. That's why this screen asks for both and a towel request doesn't.

  • Its own hours, photos and reviews

    The hotel is open all night; the studio closes at 22:00. Guests rate the studio, not the hotel. So the hours, the work photos and the reviews sit here and belong to this place alone.

  • The cost is visible before the yes

    Every service shows its price, and the total at the bottom moves with each pick. Nobody sits in the chair and then discovers the bill.

A note for the hotel

The hotel enters each place once — name, hours, location, photos and price list — and says whether it runs the place itself or an outside business does. Orders for an outside business reach that business directly, and the hotel still sees them. Two things are still open for you to decide: who collects the money, and whether "charge it to my room" is allowed for outside businesses.
