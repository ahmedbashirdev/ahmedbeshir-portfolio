Not just a list. It feels personal.

Ordering a service should feel like talking to the front desk — friendly and clear, with a real person on the other side.

01

Best services shown first

Special experiences appear at the top, like a Bedouin dinner under the stars.

02

Your room is shown

The guest sees their room number and that charges go to the room bill, so payment is clear.

03

Live order status

The guest watches the order move from "in progress" to "delivered". No worry about where it is.

04

Pick a time with one tap

The guest picks a time with one tap — Now, or a suggested time. Faster than typing.

05

A real name, not a department

The guest sees "Mariam will confirm in 2 minutes" — a real person, not a machine.

06

The button says where it goes

The button says "Send to kitchen" or "Send to housekeeping", so the guest knows who receives it.
