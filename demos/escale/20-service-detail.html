Know it before you ask.

The catalog gives a name and a price. But a spa treatment or an excursion is a real decision — the guest wants to know what's included, how long it takes, and what others felt. This screen is that step, between browsing and requesting.

  • One service, the full picture

    Just as the housekeeping screen shows staff everything about one room, this shows the guest everything about one service — what's included, how long, where, and the price.

  • Pick the option here

    The guest chooses the session — 60 min, 90 min, or couples — on this screen, so the request screen stays short and simple.

  • What's included, in plain words

    No surprises at the door. The guest sees exactly what they get before they ask for it.

  • A real guest's words

    One honest line from a past guest builds more trust than a star rating on its own.

  • Browse → detail → request

    Tapping a service in the catalog, or an offer on the Offers screen, opens this page. The request composer comes next.

A note for the hotel

The hotel writes each service once from its dashboard — description, what's included, sessions, hours, and photos. Escalé shows it here in each guest's language, for in-house guests and for visitors who open an offer.
