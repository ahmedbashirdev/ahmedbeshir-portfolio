The hotel in your pocket.

Every hotel still hands the guest a paper folder. This screen takes its place — Wi-Fi, dining, services, and hours, always up to date.

  • One place for everything

    Wi-Fi, dining, services, and hours — all on one screen, instead of a folder in the drawer.

  • For every guest in the hotel

    Any guest staying at the hotel sees this page, no matter where they booked.

  • Services are one tap away

    Tapping a service opens the request screen, so the guest can ask for it right away.

  • Only what the guest needs

    Short, clear, useful information — not a long brochure.

  • The same warm look

    It feels like the rest of the app — calm, and easy to read at a glance.

A note for the hotel

The hotel fills this page once from its dashboard — name, photos, dining venues, services, and hours. Every guest then sees the same up-to-date page, in their own language.
