Why no one else has this.

No other app in Egypt lets travel agencies and hotels meet like this. This is the screen that makes a hotel choose Escalé. We made it look like part of the same app, not a separate tool.

01

Live requests from agencies

Agencies post what they need — for example, 20 Nile-view rooms for a group. The hotel sees the budget, dates, and meal plan clearly.

02

You can trust the agency

Each agency shows its location, its partner level, and how many bookings it has made. That is stronger than just a name.

03

You can see the competition

Small avatars show how many other hotels are bidding. The hotel knows the demand is real.

04

Your bids, at a glance

"Leading", "Outbid", or "Pending" — colours tell the hotel which bids need attention now.

05

A live activity feed

"Won a 10-room bid" or "Outbid by EGP 80" — the feed keeps the hotel coming back to check.

06

One pink button

"Place bid" is the only pink button here. Pink always marks the main action.
