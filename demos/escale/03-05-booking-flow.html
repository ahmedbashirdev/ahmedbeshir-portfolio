Why the booking is only 3 steps.

Every extra screen makes people give up before they finish. We kept booking to three short steps so guests complete it.

01

You always see your progress

Three dots at the top show which step you are on and how many are left.

02

The price stays on screen

The total price is shown the whole time you book. No surprise at the payment step.

03

Adding your ID is optional

If the guest adds their ID, they get a QR code to walk straight to their room.

04

Local payment first

Paymob and Fawry are shown first because Egyptian guests know and trust them.

05

Free cancellation is clear

The free-cancellation note shows right before payment, so the guest feels safe.

06

One pink button

Steps 1 and 2 use a dark button. Only the final Confirm button is pink.
