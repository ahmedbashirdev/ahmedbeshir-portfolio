The part other apps forget.

Booking apps sell one thing: the room. But a hotel also has a restaurant, a spa, a pool — and they sit half-empty. This screen sells them, to guests and to people who live or work nearby.

  • Not just rooms

    Booking apps stop at the room. A hotel's restaurant, spa, and pool earn money too — this screen sells them.

  • Open to people outside the hotel

    Anyone near the hotel can book an offer, not only guests. New customers, and new income for the hotel.

  • One clear offer per card

    Each card shows the deal, the price, and how far the hotel is — nothing extra.

  • Easy to book

    Pick an offer, pay in the app, and show the code at the venue.

  • The same calm look

    It feels like the rest of Escalé — warm, simple, and easy to scan.

  • Everything you need before you tap

    The discount, the old price, the rating, and the opening hours are all on the card — so nobody arrives at a closed door.

A note for the hotel

The hotel adds its offers from the dashboard — a dish, a spa package, a pool pass — and sets the price and the days. Escalé shows each offer to guests inside the hotel and to people nearby.
