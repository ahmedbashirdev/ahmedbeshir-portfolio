Getting in should be the easy part.

A guest should never be stuck at the door of the app. These two screens give a warm welcome and a sign-in that anyone can finish in a few seconds.

01

A warm welcome, not a form

The first screen feels like the rest of the app — warm and calm. The guest picks how to sign in.

02

Phone sign-in works for everyone

Many guests across Egypt and the Gulf do not use Apple or Google accounts. A phone number always works, so no one is left out.

03

A short code, not a password

The guest gets a code by text and types it in. Nothing to remember, and nothing to forget.

04

Hotel-link guests start here too

A guest who booked elsewhere and got a hotel link signs in the same way. Their stay is then connected automatically.

05

Apple and Google for speed

Guests who prefer it can sign in with one tap using Apple or Google.

06

One pink button

"Verify & continue" is the only pink button. Pink always marks the final step.
