The look & feel

Escalé should feel warm, calm, and high quality. Earthy colours, clean text, and plenty of space. Not loud, and not cheap.

  • Clear, elegant text · Two carefully chosen fonts give every screen a clean, magazine-like look.
  • Warm colours · Aubergine and terracotta feel welcoming. Pink is saved for the single most important button.
  • The triangle mark · The three triangles from the logo appear softly across the app as a light decoration.
  • Room to breathe · Screens are never crowded. Empty space is used on purpose, not by accident.
